    Our very honest story

    It started because Josh hated picking up dog poop.

    Not disliked it. Hated it. The smell, the bending, watching every step, and knowing it would all need doing again a few days later.

    Then came the obvious thought: if he hated it, plenty of other dog owners did too. Busy parents, long workdays, bad backs, and anyone who'd rather spend Saturday enjoying the yard than searching it.

    So Josh and Dee built this service in DeSoto to take one gross, repetitive job off your list for good. We're growing neighborhood by neighborhood across Southern DFW so routes stay tight and service stays personal.

    "I hated picking up dog poop. Then I realized plenty of other people hated it just as much, and would gladly pay someone dependable to make it disappear."Josh Reed, founder

    FAQ

    Questions? We've got answers

    Do I need to be home during the cleanup?

    Nope! Just make sure we can get into the yard and that your dogs are inside or secured. We'll close and latch the gate when we leave.

    Is there a contract?

    Never. Recurring plans are simply prepaid month to month. Pause or cancel whenever you need to.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on your schedule and how many dogs you have. Fill out the free quote form and you'll see your price instantly. One-time cleanups are quoted individually by email.

    How will I know you came?

    You'll get a text when we're on the way and another when the yard is done, plus a photo of your closed gate.

    What if my yard has heavy buildup?

    If it's been a while, your first visit may need a one-time cleanup first. Just tell us when the yard was last cleaned and we'll include it in your quote.

    What do you do with the waste?

    We double-bag everything and place it in your outdoor trash bin, so it goes out with your regular pickup.

    What if it rains on my service day?

    Light rain doesn't stop us. For severe weather we'll reschedule and let you know right away.

    How do I get started?

    Get your free quote, then tap "Start service" and fill out a short intake form. We'll email your payment link along with your assigned service day and start date.
