FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

Do I need to be home during the cleanup?

Nope! Just make sure we can get into the yard and that your dogs are inside or secured. We'll close and latch the gate when we leave.

Is there a contract?

Never. Recurring plans are simply prepaid month to month. Pause or cancel whenever you need to.

How much does it cost?

$20 per visit for 1–2 dogs, plus $5 per visit for each additional dog. Weekly and twice-a-month plans are prepaid monthly. One-time cleanups are quoted individually, so just email us.

What do you do with the waste?

We bag everything up and haul it away with us, so there's nothing left behind in your trash can.

What if it rains on my service day?

Light rain doesn't stop us. For severe weather we'll reschedule and let you know right away.

How do I get started?

Fill out the quote form. We'll email you a payment link, and once it's paid we'll send your first scheduled cleanup date.
